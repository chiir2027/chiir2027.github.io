Student Volunteer Call

We are searching for Student Volunteers for the CHIIR 2027 conference in Berlin!

As a Student Volunteer you’ll help to ensure the conference runs smoothly, gain insights into how it’s organized, and have the opportunity to connect with researchers from around the world. In particular, you may help with:

  • Welcoming and registering participants
  • Providing directions and answering attendees’ questions
  • Supporting conference sessions and speakers
  • Helping with room setup and event logistics
  • Assisting with community activities and networking events

The admission fees for Student Volunteers will be covered by the conference organization. Furthermore, you can apply for a SIGIR student travel grant if you are presenting an accepted paper at the conference.

Only full-time students can apply. Interested? We’d love to hear from you! To apply for the CHIIR 2027 student volunteer team, please fill out the Application form and upload a short CV (not more than one page) focusing on your previous academic career. We can offer up to five student volunteer fees; the selection of volunteers will be based on a list of criteria, including the motivation statement, accepted submission, and connection to CHIIR.

Please note the following important dates:

  • Application Opens: October 25, 2026
  • Application Deadline: November 20, 2026
  • Acceptance Notifications: December 1st, 2026

For further questions, please contact our Volunteer Chair Franziska Fenz: franziska.maria.fenz@hu-berlin.de
